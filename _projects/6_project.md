Conducted at the **Origami Technology Lab**, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), this research investigates the spatial dynamics of magnetically responsive origami metamaterials.

### Background & Motivation
Origami structures, particularly Kresling cylinders, possess coupled axial and rotational motions with programmable bistable and multistable energy landscapes. By embedding magnetic moments into origami facets, wireless, untethered, and rapid actuation can be achieved via applied external magnetic fields. Modeling the full 3D spatial multi-body dynamics under magnetic torques requires robust structural representations and high-performance physics engines.

### Key Highlights
- **Spatial Bar-and-Hinge Framework:** Formulated a spatial bar-and-hinge structural network capturing panel stretching, facet bending, and crease folding stiffness to represent continuous deformations of origami facets.
- **Magnetic Actuation Formulation:** Modeled magnetic torque and force interactions between distributed magnetic moments and dynamic external magnetic fields.
- **Open-Source Newton-Physics Environment:** Implemented numerical dynamic simulations within the open-source **Newton-Physics** simulation engine, capturing rapid snapping transitions, dynamic deployment, and multistable state switching.
- **Conference Publication:** Ongoing research targeted for the *International Conference on Modern Mechanics and Applications (ICOMMA 2027)*: *"Spatial Dynamic Modeling of Magnetically Actuated Kresling Origami: A Bar-and-Hinge Framework"*.
