This research explores the complex dynamic behaviors of Kresling origami structures under dynamic harmonic loading.

### Key Highlights
- **Five-Parameter Energy Framework:** Formulated an analytical energy framework capturing coupled axial-torsional strain energy and multistable transitions.
- **Nonlinear Dynamics Simulation:** Investigated large-amplitude oscillations, intra-well vs. inter-well jumping, and chaotic responses under various frequency and amplitude conditions.
- **Bifurcation & Phase Space Analysis:** Constructed phase portraits, Poincaré maps, and bifurcation diagrams to map regions of stability and chaos.
