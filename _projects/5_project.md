In collaboration with **ICMAM Lab Collaborative**, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), this project develops Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) frameworks for the mechanics and optimization of advanced composite structures.

### Background & Motivation
Laminated composite plates are widely utilized in aerospace, automotive, and marine engineering due to their high strength-to-weight and stiffness-to-weight ratios. Analyzing and optimizing their stress distribution, deflection, and stability requires solving coupled partial differential equations (PDEs) under shear deformation theories.

### Key Highlights
- **First-order Shear Deformation Theory (FSDT):** Formulated loss functions directly embedding the kinematic relations, equilibrium equations, and boundary conditions derived from FSDT.
- **Structural Analysis via PINN:** Accurately predicted transverse deflections, in-plane stresses, and interlaminar shear stresses across multidirectional laminates without traditional mesh generation.
- **Laminate Stacking Sequence Optimization:** Established neural-based inverse optimization schemes to identify optimal fiber orientations and layer thickness distributions for targeted stiffness and buckling capacity.
