At AiRA Lab, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, this project explores Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML) methods for composite materials and structures.

### Key Highlights
- **Governing Equations:** Embedded governing differential equations of laminated composite plates directly into the neural network architecture.
- **Structural Analysis:** Analyzed stress distributions, deflections, and buckling responses under multidirectional boundary conditions and loading scenarios.
- **Design Optimization:** Developed gradient-based optimization loops using PINN surrogate models to find optimal layer orientations and thickness distributions.
