projects

A showcase of my research and engineering projects in Physics-Informed Machine Learning, robotics, and scientific computing.

research

Inverse Design of Kresling Origami via PINNs

Modeling and multi-solution inverse design of Kresling origami structures using Physics-Informed Neural Networks.

Global Dynamics and Bifurcation of Kresling Origami

Comprehensive analysis of nonlinear dynamics, multi-parameter energy frameworks, and bifurcation behaviors of Kresling origami under harmonic excitation.

Magnetically Actuated Kresling Origami Dynamics

Spatial dynamic modeling and numerical simulation of magnetically actuated Kresling origami via a bar-and-hinge framework and Newton-Physics engine at Origami Technology Lab (HUST).

Molecular Dynamics Simulation & AI for DNA Origami

Coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations and deep learning for the structural prediction and inverse design of DNA origami nanostructures at AiRA Lab.

PINN for Composite Laminates Analysis & Optimization

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN) for structural analysis and design optimization of composite laminates based on First-order Shear Deformation Theory (FSDT) with ICMAM Lab Collaborative (HCMUT).

Dynamics of 3-RRR Planar Parallel Robot

Kinematic simulation, dynamic modeling, and trajectory-tracking algorithms for parallel mechanisms in MATLAB/Simulink.
