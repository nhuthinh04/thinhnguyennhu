### Teaching Assistant

#### **AI for Science and Engineering (AI4SE)**
- **Role:** Teaching Assistant
- **Institution:** AiRA Lab, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST)
- **Course Repository:** [github.com/AiRA-Laboratory/AI4SE](https://github.com/AiRA-Laboratory/AI4SE)
- **Format:** Lectures, hands-on lab sessions, and coding tutorials in Python, PyTorch, and JAX.

##### Course Overview
This course provides students with fundamental and advanced knowledge at the intersection of artificial intelligence and physical/engineering sciences, focusing on how physics laws can be integrated into modern deep learning models.

##### Core Topics Covered:
- **Physics-Informed Machine Learning (PIML):** Principles of embedding governing differential equations, boundary conditions, and physical invariants into neural network architectures.
- **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs):** Automatic differentiation, loss formulation, residuals of PDEs/ODEs, and multi-objective optimization for forward and inverse problems.
- **Neural Operators:** Function-to-function mappings for PDE solving across variable resolutions (e.g., Fourier Neural Operators - FNO, Deep Operator Networks - DeepONet).
- **Scientific ML Workflows:** High-performance computing for scientific discovery with PyTorch and JAX.
- **Engineering Applications:** Structural inverse design, metamaterial behavior prediction, fluid dynamics, and multiscale modeling.
